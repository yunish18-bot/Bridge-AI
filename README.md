
# BRIDGE AI

## CSC 425 - Artificial Intelligence
Northern Kentucky University | Fall 2026

## Project Description
BRIDGE AI is an AI-powered assistant designed to help
international students understand important parts of
studying and living in the United States.

The application will focus on three main features:

1. Education and Finances
   - Explain the U.S. college education system.
   - Explain basic credit cards and credit scores.

2. Career Skills
   - Compare student skills with job requirements.
   - Suggest skills the student should learn.

3. Job Application Tracker
   - Track applications, interviews, and deadlines.
   - Remind students about follow-up actions.

## AI Approach
We plan to use rule-based reasoning to provide
recommendations based on information entered by students.

We may also explore search algorithms to identify
useful learning paths for career development.
